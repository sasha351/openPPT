Quick flag: the FoxPro "notes" field is actually three overloaded columns
mashed together with pipe delimiters depending on which decade the row was
written in. Mei and I spent yesterday reverse-engineering the encoding.

Decision: we're writing a normalization pass that splits this into
structured fields where possible and dumps the rest into a free-text
"legacy_notes" column rather than trying to fully parse 20 years of formats.
Good enough beats perfect here — Carlos, flag this to QA as an accepted
lossy transform, not a bug.

— Dave
